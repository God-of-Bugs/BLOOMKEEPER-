# AI DISCLOSURE STATEMENT

## Project: BLOOMKEEPER

This project was created for Godot India Community Mini Jam.

### AI Usage Summary
- **Human Creator & Ownership**: Mayank Kumar led all creative direction, game design, decision-making, and overall vision.
- **AI Coding Assistance**: AI coding assistants (Antigravity, Ziva, and ChatGPT) were used as development aids for technical planning, GDScript logic, scene assembly, procedural gameplay visuals, and verification. The human creator reviewed the project and retained creative direction and final decisions.
- **Asset Usage**: All 3D visuals utilize native Godot primitive geometry meshes (`CapsuleMesh`, `BoxMesh`, `SphereMesh`, `CylinderMesh`) and built-in materials, composed procedurally in GDScript; no third-party art assets are included.
